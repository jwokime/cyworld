Following 9/11, the U.S. has spent over $8 trillion on wars in the Middle East, and in 2023 alone, the U.S. spent around $961 billion on defense—more than the combined defense budgets of 31 NATO allies. 

One of the first data/website projects I made at the Columbia Journalism School's summer program for data journalism. Made with Mapbox, the Military Intervention Project dataset, and many sleepless nights.
